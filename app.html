    Good to know

    💵 Paying at the stalls
    • All stalls accept cash only.
    • Carry small notes and coins so students can give change quickly.
    • Stalls accept Mela coupons only (₹10, ₹20, ₹50, ₹100). Buy them with cash at the Coupon Counter near the entry gate. No UPI.
    • Use My Mela list in the shop to see how many coupons you will need.
    🙋 Help desk, lost and found

    ⛑️ First aid

    🔐 Your privacy
    • This app stores nothing about you on any server.
    • Your shopping list stays on your own phone.
    • No stall collects your personal details or photographs you without consent.
    📲 Add this app to your phone
    • Android: tap the browser menu (⋮), then Install app or Add to Home screen.
    • iPhone: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
    • Once added, it opens like an app and works even with weak network.
    🏫 About the Department of Skill and Innovation

    The Department of Skill and Innovation (DSI) at Army Public School Jorhat leads hands-on skill education and technology projects for students. The Kaushal Mela 2026 app, website and technology showcase are built in-house by DSI.
